Questions

Pricing questions, answered.

Can I try Stylisee before I commit?

Yes. Every plan begins with a 14-day free trial, with no credit card required. Explore the workspace, add your services, and see how it fits your practice.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Your practice changes as it grows, and your plan can change with it. Move up or down as your business changes.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contracts. Choose monthly or yearly billing, and save 20% when you pay yearly.

Which currency will I be charged in?

All plans are priced in Australian dollars (AUD). The currency selector shows an approximate conversion to help you compare. Your bank or card provider sets the final exchange rate.

Does Stylisee work for colour analysts and image consultants?

Yes. Client profiles capture colour season, body shape, skin tone, hair colour, style preferences and risk responses, so your own method has a place to live.

Is my clients' data secure?

Each business's data is kept separate and is only visible to you and the team members you invite. Read our privacy policy and security pages for details.
