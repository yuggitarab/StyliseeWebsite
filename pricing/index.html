Questions

Pricing questions, answered.

How does the pilot work?

Apply through our contact form and we'll set up your account with complimentary access to the full app for 60 days. There is no card to enter and nothing is charged. You'll see how many days remain inside the app.

What happens when the pilot ends?

Your clients, bookings and settings are kept safe. You'll be able to continue on a paid plan once billing opens, and we'll let you know before anything changes.

Which currency will I be charged in?

All plans are priced in Australian dollars (AUD). The currency selector shows an approximate conversion to help you compare. Your bank or card provider sets the final exchange rate.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Start with the plan that suits your practice today and move up or down as your business changes.

Does Stylisee work for colour analysts and image consultants?

Yes. Client profiles capture colour tones, skin tone, hair colour, body shape, style preferences and risk responses, so your own method has a place to live.

Is my clients' data secure?

Each business's data is kept separate and is only visible to you and the team members you invite. Read our privacy policy and security pages for details.
