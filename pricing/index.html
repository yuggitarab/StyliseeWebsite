Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I see current plan prices?

Plans, features, and pricing are displayed inside the Stylisee application so they are always up to date. Sign in or create a free account to view and compare plans.

Can I change plans after signing up?

Yes. You can change your plan at any time from your account settings inside the application. Visit the app for details on how plan changes are handled.

Is my client data safe if I change plans?

Your data is always yours. Client records, bookings, and styling data are retained in your account regardless of the plan you are on.

Who handles billing and payments?

Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. Stylisee does not store your card details. For billing questions, contact billing@stylisee.com.
