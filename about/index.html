About Stylisee

Our Mission

Stylisee was built with one goal: to give personal stylists, image consultants, and colour analysts the professional tools they deserve. We believe that creative professionals should spend their time doing what they love — transforming how people look and feel — not wrestling with spreadsheets, scattered notes, and disjointed booking systems.

What We Do

Stylisee is a comprehensive business management platform purpose-built for the styling industry. From managing client profiles with detailed colour analysis and body shape data, to scheduling appointments, cataloguing services, tracking revenue, and gathering feedback — everything lives in one elegant, secure workspace.

Built for Style Professionals

Unlike generic business tools, Stylisee understands the unique needs of styling professionals. Our platform supports detailed style profiling — capturing colour seasons, skin tones, hair colour, body shape, and personal style preferences. Whether you are conducting a full wardrobe audit, planning a shopping trip, or documenting a colour analysis session, Stylisee helps you keep every detail organised and accessible.

Your Data, Your Business

We take data privacy seriously. Every business on Stylisee operates in its own isolated environment — your client data, photos, wardrobe notes, bookings, and analytics are completely separate from every other business on the platform. Only you and the team members you explicitly invite can access your information.

Our Values

  • Simplicity — Powerful features wrapped in an intuitive, beautiful interface.
  • Privacy — Your data and your clients' data are protected and never shared.
  • Reliability — A platform you can depend on for your day-to-day business.
  • Industry Focus — Built specifically for styling professionals, not adapted from generic tools.
