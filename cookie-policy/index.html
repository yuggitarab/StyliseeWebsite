Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember information about your visit, such as whether you are signed in, making your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

How We Use Cookies

Stylisee uses only essential cookies that are strictly necessary for the platform to function. We do not use any advertising, marketing, or third-party tracking cookies.

Cookies We Use

Cookie Purpose Duration
Session cookie Keeps you signed in while using the platform Until you sign out or close your browser
Authentication token Securely identifies your account during your session Session duration
Theme preference Remembers your light/dark mode preference (stored locally on your device) Persistent (local storage)
Stripe cookies Set by Stripe during payment processing to prevent fraud and verify transactions. Only present during checkout flows. Session / short-term

Third-Party Cookies — Stripe

When you interact with subscription payment flows, Stripe, Inc. may set cookies on your device for fraud prevention, security verification, and transaction integrity purposes. These cookies are governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy. Stylisee does not control these cookies and they are not used for advertising or tracking purposes.

What We Do Not Use

  • Third-party analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics)
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Social media tracking cookies
  • Cross-site tracking of any kind

Managing Cookies

Since we only use essential cookies required for the platform to function, disabling them may prevent you from using Stylisee. You can manage cookies through your browser settings — most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies from specific websites.

Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at privacy@stylisee.com.
